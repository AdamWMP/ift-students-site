Questions

Straight answers

How long does the course take?

The Cert is an 8-week qualification. The Career and Business pathways run longer, but all qualify you to the same EQF Level 4 standard.

Do I need to be fit or experienced already?

No. Every cohort includes complete beginners. The course teaches anatomy, programming and coaching from the ground up.

What does it cost — and can I pay in instalments?

Payment plans are available. We'll walk you through the options and find what works for you on the call.

Will I actually get clients after I qualify?

IFT has 200+ active hiring partners across Ireland. Graduate with a merit or distinction and you unlock placement opportunities with our network of gym partners — many students receive offers during placement, before they've even graduated.

Is the qualification recognised?

Yes — a REPs Ireland approved PT qualification at EQF Level 4, also EHFA approved and Skillnet Ireland supported.
