## What Irish fitness audiences actually watch

Irish fitness audiences don’t want American-style hype.

They want:
- practical advice
- realistic results
- no fluff
- humour
- authenticity
- beginner-friendly tone

The content that performs best in Ireland usually fits one of these:
1) “Stop doing this” mistakes content
2) “Do this instead” simplification content
3) Weekend strategy content
4) Protein and nutrition basics
5) Gym confidence for beginners
6) Transformation proof
7) Irish lifestyle relatability

If you make content that feels like Ireland, you win.

## The 3 content pillars that dominate the algorithm

Pillar 1: Education (saves)
Educational content gets saved because it feels useful.

Examples:
- “3 ways to eat more protein in Ireland”
- “Why your steps matter more than cardio”
- “How to train for fat loss without burning out”

Pillar 2: Proof (shares)
Proof content gets shared because it inspires belief.

Examples:
- client wins
- habit streaks
- strength progress
- transformation journeys

Pillar 3: Personality (follows)
Personality content builds connection.

Examples:
- Irish gym humour
- day in the life
- behind the scenes coaching
- “things only Irish gym-goers understand”

If you rotate these three pillars, you grow faster.

Hook writing: the micro-skill that changes everything

Your hook is your first 1–2 seconds.

If your hook is weak, nobody watches.

Hooks that work in Ireland:

1) “If you’re doing X and not getting results…”
“If you’re training 4x/week and still not losing fat…”

2) “Stop doing this if your goal is X…”
“Stop doing this if fat loss is your goal…”

3) “Most people in Ireland get this wrong…”
“Most people in Ireland underestimate protein…”

4) “Here’s the simplest way to…”
“Here’s the simplest way to stay consistent…”

5) “This is why you’re stuck…”
“This is why your progress keeps stalling…”

Hooks don’t need to be dramatic.
They need to be clear.

Video structure that keeps attention

Use this simple structure:

1) Hook (1–2 seconds)
2) Problem (5 seconds)
3) Solution (10–25 seconds)
4) Call to action (2 seconds)

Example:
Hook: “If you’re doing everything right and still not losing fat…”
Problem: “Your weekends might be wiping out your weekly deficit.”
Solution: “Here’s a simple weekend strategy…”
CTA: “DM me ‘PLAN’ if you want help.”

That’s it.

Posting schedule that’s sustainable for Irish trainers

Most coaches burn out because they try to post too much too soon.

A sustainable plan:
- 3 reels per week
- 1–3 stories per day
- 1 educational carousel per week

A growth-focused plan:
- 5 reels per week
- daily stories
- weekly proof post

Consistency beats intensity.

## How to turn views into clients (the conversion system)

Views don’t pay bills.
Clients do.

So you need a conversion system.

Step 1: Content should align with your offer
If your offer is fat loss coaching, 80% of your content should be fat loss related.

Step 2: Use soft CTAs
Irish clients hate pressure.

Examples:
- “Message me START if you want a plan.”
- “If you want help, DM me COACH.”
- “Want the checklist? Send me GUIDE.”

Step 3: Have a simple DM script
Ask:
- goal
- obstacle
- timeline
Then invite them to a call or application.

Step 4: Show proof consistently
Proof is what converts attention into trust.
