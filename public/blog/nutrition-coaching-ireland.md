## The 5 nutrition principles that work best in Ireland (fat loss + recomposition)

Principle 1: Protein is the anchor
If you get clients eating enough protein, everything improves:
- appetite control
- muscle retention
- recovery
- body composition
- satiety

Simple Irish protein targets:
- Women: 100–140g/day (depending on size/activity)
- Men: 140–180g/day (depending on size/activity)

You don’t need to overwhelm them.
Set a range and build habits.

Principle 2: Calorie awareness without obsession
Many clients in Ireland don’t need macro perfection.

They need calorie awareness.

Teach:
- portion control
- high-volume foods
- consistent meal structure
- “calorie creep” at weekends

For some clients, tracking works.
For others, it becomes obsessive.

A good coach chooses the right tool for the person.

Principle 3: Daily movement is the multiplier (steps)
Irish clients often overestimate how active they are.

Steps are the fat loss multiplier.

A simple goal:
- build toward 7k–10k daily average

And create a winter-proof plan:
- short walks
- treadmill incline
- movement snacks
Because Irish weather can crush motivation.

Principle 4: Weekend strategy beats weekday perfection
Teach clients a weekend structure:
- protein before social events
- choose one “main indulgence”
- drink water between drinks
- walk the next day
- don’t punish Monday with extremes

This turns weekend chaos into weekend control.

Principle 5: Consistency over restriction
The Irish client who succeeds isn’t the one who goes extreme.

It’s the one who stays consistent for months.

Your coaching should build:
- habits
- routines
- identity

Not temporary punishment.

## How to coach nutrition within scope (important in Ireland)

As a PT or coach, you must stay in your scope.

That means:
- education
- guidance
- habit support
- general nutrition coaching
- behaviour change strategies

Not:
- medical nutrition therapy
- diagnosing conditions
- prescribing treatment

In Ireland, clients appreciate honesty.

Say:
“I can support you with structure and habits. If you need medical support, we’ll work alongside your GP or dietitian.”

This builds trust.

## Common nutrition mistakes Irish clients make (and how to coach them)

Mistake 1: “Eating healthy” but overeating portions
Fix: portion education, protein targets, hunger awareness

Mistake 2: Under-eating protein
Fix: simple swaps, protein with every meal

Mistake 3: Weekend binge-restrict cycle
Fix: weekend planning + Monday reset routine

Mistake 4: “Calories don’t matter”
Fix: gentle education, weekly averages

Mistake 5: Overestimating exercise calorie burn
Fix: teach reality, focus on food structure
