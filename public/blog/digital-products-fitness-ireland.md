## What counts as a digital product in fitness?

A digital product is any paid resource that delivers value without live 1:1 sessions.

Examples:
- 6-week beginner programme PDF
- home workout plan
- gym confidence blueprint
- recipe pack
- meal prep guide (Irish-friendly)
- macro calculator template
- habit tracker
- 30-day challenge
- mini-course on fat loss basics
- training templates for specific goals

## The key idea:
It’s not “passive income.”
It’s “scalable income.”

You build it once, then sell it many times.

## How to pick the right digital product

Use this filter:
- What problem do people constantly ask you about?
- What result can you deliver in 30–60 days?
- What is the simplest possible system?

Example:
If your niche is busy professionals, your product might be:
“3-Day Busy Professional Strength Plan + Meal Structure Guide.”

Simple sells.

Pricing digital products in Ireland

Most Irish trainers price too low or too high.

A realistic ladder:
- €17–€29: small guides, templates
- €39–€79: full programmes
- €99–€199: mini-courses or challenges
- €200+: advanced courses (rare, but possible)

Start simple.
Grow based on demand.
