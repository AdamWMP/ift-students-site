## What “high-ticket” actually means in Ireland

High-ticket is a relative term.

In Ireland, “high-ticket” coaching typically means:
- €300–€600 per month (premium hybrid coaching)
- €500–€1,500 per programme (transformations, specialist programmes)
- €1,500–€3,000 per 12–16 week premium coaching (more rare, but possible for advanced offers)

But here’s the key:

High-ticket isn’t about charging more for the same service.

It’s about offering a higher level of support and a clearer outcome.

If you charge more but deliver the same experience, you get:
- cancellations
- complaints
- refund requests
- reputation damage

High-ticket works when you build a premium coaching system that feels worth it.

## Why Irish clients pay premium prices (and why they don’t)

Irish clients will pay more when:
- the outcome is specific
- the plan is clear
- the coach feels trustworthy
- the experience feels professional
- there’s proof and reassurance
- the coach communicates like a human

Irish clients won’t pay premium prices when:
- the offer is vague (“get fit”)
- it feels generic (“custom plan” with no explanation)
- it feels pushy or “salesy”
- the coach seems unqualified or unclear
- the messaging feels like influencer hype

## The Irish market rewards:

## How to package your coaching so it feels premium

High-ticket coaches sell outcomes.

Examples:
- “a structured plan you can follow even when work gets busy”
- “simple targets that fit your lifestyle, weekends included”
- “weekly accountability so you never drift for weeks without progress”

## The “Irish-friendly” sales process (no pushiness required)

1) Ask questions
2) Reflect back what you heard
3) Explain the plan (simply)
4) Present the best-fit package
5) Invite them to decide
